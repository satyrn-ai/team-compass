# SatrynAI/satyrn-ai/satyrn.ai Code of Conduct

We aim to make our community welcoming, safe, and respectful.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the dignity, rights, and contributions of all individuals. Of course, such a diversity of contributors will lead to a diversity of perspectives. Healthy disgreement will naturally occur, but disagreement is never an excuse for aggression. Our Code of Conduct (CoC) is an effort to ensure a safe space for all contributions. Please take this document in the spirit it is intended, rather than an exhustive list of permitted of prohibited behaviours.

## Expected Behaviours

We agree to behave mindfully toward each other and act in ways that centre our shared values, including:

* Being friendly and welcoming.
* Engaging kindly and honestly with others.
* Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
* Interacting and communicating with patience and empathy.
* Taking responsibility for our actions and contributions.
* Gracefully giving and accepting constructive feedback.
* Committing to repairing harm when it occurs.
* Respecting consent and boundaries. If someone says "no", asks you to stop, or sets a boundary, respect it immediately and without debate.

Impact matters more than intent. Even when harm is unintentional, we are all responsible for the impact of our words and actions. If someone tells you that your behavior has caused harm, listen with empathy and take responsibility for making things right. This doesn't always mean a formal process is necessary, often clear communication will resolve an issue. If formal action is needed, please see the [Reporting](#reporting) section below.

## Inappropriate Behaviours

The following are violations of this Code of Conduct (this is not an exhaustive list):

* Harassment - violating expressed boundaries or engaging in unwanted personal attention
* Character attacks - insulting or demeaning comments directed at a community member or group
* Stereotyping - characterising anyone's personality or behaviour on the basis of their identity or traits
* Microaggressions - subtle behaviours or comments that communicate hostile or derogatory attitudes toward marginalised groups
* Sexualisation - behaving in a way that would be considered inappropriately intimate
* Violating confidentiality - sharing someone's private information without permission, including doxxing
* Violent behaviours - causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or harm toward any person or group
* Discouraging involvement - acting in ways that push others out of the community
* Unsolicited DMs - sending uninvited direct messages to community members
* Retaliation - taking adverse action against someone for making a CoC report

Community members asked to stop any restricted behaviour are expected to comply immediately.

## Reporting

If you witness or experience a violation, please report it to the CoC team at [maybe we get a coc email?]. All reports will be handled with confidentiality and care.

## Consequences

Violations may result in the following, depending on severity and context:

* Warning: a private written warning from the CoC team
* Temporary limited access: a cooldown period from specific channels or interactions
* Temporary suspension: removal from community spaces for a defined period
* Permanent ban: removal from all community spaces

The CoC team will use their discretion and judgement in all cases. This ladder is a guideline, not a rigid formula.
