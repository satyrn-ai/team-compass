# Discord channels

Find the right channel for your conversation in the Satyrn Discord server.

All channels are visible to all members except the two private channels, #orgs-private and #infra-sec-private. These are used only in limited circumstances where privacy or confidentiality need to be considered, and are accessible by the organisers.

## Start here

| --- | --- |
| #welcome-guide | This channel is your starting point for the Satyrn Discord server. It contains information on our code of conduct, how to get involved, and some Discord basics. |

## Community

| Channel | Description |
| --- | --- |
| #announcements | This channel is for announcements, like team updates, meeting calls, important information. Only the organisers can post in this channel. If you'd like to start a discussion related to your announcement I suggest starting a topic in #general-topics. |
| #introductions | Introduce yourself here! Say hi, and perhaps share a bit about yourself, what brought you to Satyrn and what you're interested in. |
| #general-chat | This Text channel is for general discussion: an ask for help, something that doesn't align to any particular team channel, a place to post things (Satyrn-related) of interest to the whole community. Text channels are best for quick, free-flowing conversation. |
| #general-topics | This Forum channel is for general discussion, but each post is based around one particular topic. Forum channels are best for organised, specific posts, that you may want to search for or return to. |
| #appreciation-station | Give thanks and recognition. This channel is the place to give a high five, hat tip, and express appreciation for members of the community who you want to thank. Maybe they gave good PR feedback, maybe they gave your question a thoughtful answer, maybe they implemented a cool new feature... Share a shout out! |
| #random | Pet pics, holiday photos, cool articles, interesting podcasts... A place for anything and everything that isn't strictly Satyrn (within the Code of Conduct rules, of course!). |

## Organisation

| Channel | Description |
| --- | --- |
| #orgs | This Text channel is for discussion of operation/organisation related issues. Text channels are best for quick, free-flowing conversation. |
| #orgs-team-compass | A place to discuss things related to our [team-compass](https://github.com/satyrn-ai/team-compass). This includes governance, team structure and responsibilities, team meetings, and generally how we work. |
| #orgs-topics | This Forum channel is for organisation discussion. Each post is based around one particular topic. Forum channels are best for organised, specific posts, that you may want to search for or return to. |
| #orgs-private | This Text channel is for discussion of operation/organisation related issues, that need to be kept private. We aim to have all conversation publicly (go to #orgs), but recognise there may be times when issues need to be discussed with a limited audience - in this case, the organisers team. |
